# Custom Blocks

Gathering Chunks introduces several specialized machines to help manage and expand your world.

## World Forge

The World Forge is the primary crafting station for Chunk Spawners. It allows you to combine World Cores with other materials to create themed spawners.

## World Scanner

The World Scanner allows you to see information about nearby chunks, including their biome and potential resource density. It is essential for planning your expansion.

## World Mender

The World Mender can be used to repair unstable chunks or "heal" parts of the world that have been damaged or partially erased.

## World Eraser / Chunk Eraser

The World Eraser allows you to remove existing chunks. Use this with caution, as it will delete all blocks and items within that chunk, returning it to the void.

## Chunk Engine

The Chunk Engine is a powerful machine that can automate the placement of Chunk Spawners. When provided with fuel (World Cores) and spawners, it will gradually expand the world in a specified direction.
