# Getting Started with Gathering Chunks

Gathering Chunks is a unique Minecraft mod where players start their journey in a single isolated chunk surrounded by the void. Your goal is to gather resources, craft specialized items, and expand your world chunk by chunk.

## Core Gameplay Loop

Begin your journey by gathering resources from your initial chunk to craft World Cores. By combining these cores with materials like copper, you can create Chunk Spawners. Place these spawners in the void and activate them to generate new adjacent chunks, gradually rebuilding the world around you.

## Your First Chunk

When you spawn, you will be on a single 16x16 chunk. Everything you need to start is contained within this initial area.

### Initial Resources

1. Gather Wood: Most starting chunks will have a tree. This is your primary resource for crafting initial tools.
2. Collect Fragments: As you break blocks or defeat mobs, you may find World Fragments. These are the building blocks of world expansion.
3. Mining: Dig down to find ores. Be careful not to fall off the edge!

## Expanding the World

To see more of the world, you must use Chunk Spawners.

1. Craft a World Core: This requires combining fragments and shards (see the Progression guide).
2. Use a Chunk Spawner: Place a Chunk Spawner at the edge of your current chunk to generate a new adjacent chunk.
3. Unstable Chunks: Some spawners might generate unstable chunks which contain different resources or challenges.

## Progression Helper

If you find yourself stuck and unable to craft a World Core, the mod includes an automatic Progression Helper. By default, this is disabled, but when enabled, it will check if you have enough materials to progress. If you are truly stuck, it will provide a Chunk Spawner to help you continue your journey.

The helper checks both your inventory and nearby containers (within 64 blocks) to ensure you aren't stuck.
