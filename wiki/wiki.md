## Mod Overview

Gathering Chunks is a world-management and survival mod that revolves around manual world expansion. Players must work to unlock new chunks, manage resources carefully, and utilize specialized machinery to thrive in a void-based world.

### Key Improvements in this Fork

- Command-based configuration management
- Advanced progression helper with container support
- Full Fabric and NeoForge compatibility
- Improved server-side performance for world generation
- Flexible world management tools like the Chunk Eraser
