# Progression System

Gathering Chunks uses a tiered progression system based on World Fragments. Understanding how to refine these materials is key to expanding your world.

## Tiered Materials

World materials follow a conversion ratio of 4:1 as you move up the tiers.

1. World Fragment: The basic tier, drop from blocks and mobs.
2. World Shard: Crafted from 4 World Fragments.
3. World Crystal: Crafted from 4 World Shards (16 Fragments).
4. World Core: Crafted from 4 World Crystals (64 Fragments).

World Cores are the primary power source for Chunk Spawners and most custom machinery.

## Crafting Recipes

All tiered materials can be crafted in a standard crafting table.

- 4 Fragments -> 1 Shard
- 4 Shards -> 1 Crystal
- 4 Crystals -> 1 Core

Similarly, you can break down higher tiers into lower tiers in the crafting table.

## The World Forge

Later in the game, the World Forge allows you to automate or simplify some of these processes, and it is used for crafting advanced chunk spawners.
